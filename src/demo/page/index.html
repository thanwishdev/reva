This page is REVA's own. Its heading rises on load, the dot settles on a spring, the cards lift on hover, the switch slides on click and the bar at the top follows the scroll.

REVA records an element frame by frame while something happens to the page, then explains the motion as a named law with its values: a tween with its duration and easing, a spring with its stiffness and damping, a scroll link with the range it follows. Every number says how it was found: read from the page, fitted from the frames, or unknown.

The reading-progress bar above is a scroll link: its width is the share of the page you have scrolled.
